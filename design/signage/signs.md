# Store signs: the full list (#111)

Every sign the store needs, area by area, the way a customer walks in and then the staff side. Facts used: the Canfield floor plans in Dash (departments, 5 registers, glass cases, the office, break rooms, security room, Processing, Restoration and Testing rooms), store hours from Dash (`online_sales.hours`, the same text ecothrift.us shows: "9 AM - 6 PM, Monday - Saturday · Closed Sunday"), the Thrift+ rulebook, the legal memo, and the research in [`research/`](research/).

**Priority:** 1 = asked for by Bill or Jes, or needed by law or by the legal memo; 2 = recommended; 3 = nice to have. **Sizes** fit the Canon PRO-100 (up to 13 x 19 in): Letter 8.5 x 11, Tabloid 11 x 17, Poster 13 x 19. Outdoor signs cannot be inkjet paper: they go to a print shop (aluminum or plastic) or are laminated as a stopgap. **Copies** are a first count from the floor plan; Jes corrects them on the walk.

Legal research: [`research/trespass.md`](research/trespass.md) (no trespassing) and [`research/required-notices.md`](research/required-notices.md) (everything else the law asks a store to post).

## Not on the list, on purpose

- **Exit signs, fire extinguisher signs, first aid, wet floor:** bought fixtures (lit or code-standard), not printed.
- **Fitting room, donations, deliveries, "we buy":** only if they exist; see the questions. A "we buy" sign needs an Omaha secondhand dealer permit if we buy jewelry, electronics, bicycles and the like from the public.
