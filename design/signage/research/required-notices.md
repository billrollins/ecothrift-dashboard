# Signs and notices the law asks for (Eco-Thrift, 8425 West Center Road, Omaha)

Confidence: **A** = primary text read, **B** = agency statement or secondary source only, **C** = not confirmed. Statutes: `https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=<section>`. Omaha: Municode, through Ord. 44760 (2026-08-04). Fire code: Omaha adopts the IFC 2024 (OMC 46-1).

| Sign | Required? | Exact words or rule | Source | Conf. |
|------|-----------|---------------------|--------|-------|
| No smoking / no vaping | **Not required** for a general store by state law (smoking and vaping indoors are banned; the store must "take necessary and appropriate steps"). The fire official may order signs; signs at every main entrance then cover the inside. Free DHHS door decals: 1-877-633-7331, dhhs.smokefree@nebraska.gov | DHHS: "The law only requires signage for cigar shops and tobacco retail outlets, but other businesses may post signs" | NRS 71-5727, 71-5729, 71-5731; https://dhhs.ne.gov/Pages/Smoke-Free-FAQ.aspx; IFC 310.3 | A |
| No concealed handguns | **Optional, Bill's choice.** Posting is what makes concealed carry by the public unlawful on open premises (or staff asking the person to remove it) | "conspicuous notice that carrying a concealed handgun is prohibited"; no size, wording or symbol prescribed. A gun locked in a car in the lot stays legal. Omaha's own gun ordinances were repealed in 2023 (state preemption, NRS 13-330) | NRS 28-1202.01(2), (5), (6) | A |
| Staff postings (break room) | **Yes**, official free posters (we print, we don't design) | FLSA, OSHA (at least 8.5 x 14 in), EPPA, USERRA; EEOC Know Your Rights (15+ staff); FMLA (50+ staff); Nebraska minimum wage (4+); NEOC fair employment (15+); **Nebraska Paid Sick Time (11+)**; Nebraska UI benefit rights; **Omaha minimum wage notice (every Omaha employer, OMC 39-4(c), Ord. 44716 of 7-14-26; the city publishes it by Dec 1 each year)**; minors' hours notice only if anyone under 16 works | https://dol.nebraska.gov/LaborStandards/RequiredPosters · https://www.dol.gov/general/topics/posters · https://www.eeoc.gov/poster · https://dol.nebraska.gov/webdocs/Resources/Items/Poster%20Paid%20Sick%20Time.pdf | A (UI poster rule B; Omaha notice URL not found) |
| Restroom door signs | **Yes.** Omaha: "Men's"/"Women's" (or "Gentlemen"/"Ladies") in letters at least 2 in high, or the international symbols; a unisex room says "Unisex Restroom" (2 in) and needs an occupied / unoccupied sign. "Restrooms shall be available to customers." ADA: a permanent room sign needs raised uppercase sans-serif letters, Grade 2 braille, non-glare, on the latch side, 48 to 60 in up: **buy it, a printed sheet cannot comply** | OMC 49-704(c)(e), 49-705(a), 49-708(c); 2010 ADA Standards 216.2, 703 (https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/2010-stds/) | A |
| Employees only (doors) | A permanent room-name sign falls under ADA 216.2 the same way (tactile + braille) for new work; existing stores remove barriers "where readily achievable". A printed "Employees only" note is fine as an informational sign (216.3: non-glare, contrast) | 2010 ADA 216.2, 216.3, 703.5; 28 CFR 36.304 | A (reading of the text) |
| Sales tax permit | **Yes: displayed** | "conspicuously displayed at the place for which issued" | NRS 77-2705(5) | A |
| "THIS DOOR TO REMAIN UNLOCKED WHEN THIS SPACE IS OCCUPIED" | **Only if** the main door locks with a key on the inside | exact words, letters 1 in high, contrasting, durable, on or next to the door, inside | IFC 2024 1010.2.4 item 3.3 | A |
| "ELECTRICAL ROOM" | **Only if** there is an electrical panel room | "ELECTRICAL ROOM" or similar approved wording on its doors | IFC 2024 603.4.1 | A |
| Exit signs | Yes where the layout needs them: **lit fixtures listed to UL 924**, not printed | | IFC 2024 1013 | A |
| Occupant load | Only for assembly rooms; not for a store floor (the fire official decides the occupancy) | | IFC 2024 1004.9 | A |
| Return policy | No posting statute; the Nebraska Attorney General says a store with no returns "must disclose this policy". The legal memo already asks for it at the register and on the floor (S5, S6) | | https://ago.nebraska.gov/node/1657 | B |
| Video surveillance | **No notice required** (none found). Never film restrooms or fitting rooms (Class IV felony). Recording **audio** is under the wiretap law (one-party consent; a store camera is not a party) | | NRS 28-311.08; 86-290, 86-283 | A (statutes); B (no notice rule) |
| Shoplifting | **No posting.** The merchant may detain on probable cause. The civil-penalty law was **repealed in 2019**, so a sign must not threaten civil penalties | | NRS 29-402.01; 25-21,194 (repealed, LB71 2019) | A |
| "We buy" sign | Unlawful without an Omaha secondhand dealer permit **if** the store buys "regulated property" from the public (jewelry, watches, sterling, firearms, golf clubs, bicycles, electronics, video games, musical instruments, coins); used furniture stores, used clothing stores and charities taking donations are excluded | | OMC 30-21, 30-30 | A (whether it applies to us: C) |
