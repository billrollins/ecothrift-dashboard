v2.305.0 — Home and HR: Time clock, HR tab, Profile, HR workspace

User-facing theme: **Home and HR, steps 2 to 5 of the plan: Time clock replaces Today, HR and Profile are rebuilt for staff, and the HR workspace is for managers and the owner, with one Requests inbox and the Schedule builder (Dash Updates #137, #138, #139, #140).**

> **For Bill:** on Home, **Time clock** is the clock card with this week's hours, then **Mine** (this pay period: pay so far and expected, your shifts, fix a shift, time off) or **Everyone** (the week, read only). **Routines** has the to-do list and runs a routine right there. **HR** is each person's own HR: files to sign and signed, Training and SOPs (coming soon), their requests with where each stands, and the onboarding banner while they are new. **Profile** is new: photo (tap to take one), contact details, work details and the next review. In the sidebar, the **HR** workspace is now for managers and you only. It opens on **Requests**: days off, new times, shift fixes, incident reports and questions in one list, each answered right on its row. **Schedule** is the only place the week is changed, and **Incidents** lists the reports to follow up. On a phone, the bottom bar has the Home tabs, so the green band no longer covers its title with a second row of tabs.

### Changed (Home > HR for staff, Dash Update #138; decisions 5 and 8 of .ai/extended/home-and-hr-structure.md)
- The HR tab on Dash Home is now the staff member's own HR page (was five tiles linking out): **Files** (documents waiting for their signature first, then everything they signed, newest first, each opening its PDF: e-sign documents, the signed offer letter, every handbook version, signed check-ins; a new handbook version shows as "Read and sign"), **Training** and **SOPs** (coming soon), and **Requests** (Time off, Fix a shift, Report an incident, then each request with its status and the answer once there is one; an incident opens its report). Everyone sees only their own, managers included. English and Spanish.
- The flows are the existing ones: the schedule's day off / new times dialog, Hours & pay's time change (after a "Which shift?" list of their last 14 shifts) and HR > Incidents' report form. HR questions are no longer started here (Bill: issues go to him); questions already asked still show with their answer.
- A banner on top while their onboarding is in progress (how far along, the handbook to sign), or a check-in coming up this week or signed in the last two weeks: the same rules as Today's banner, which can go once Time clock is rebuilt.
- Built from the page kit on the Eco-Thrift theme (`pages/home/HrTab.tsx`). On a desk the Home band names the tab; on a phone the top bar does and the band is left out (its tab bar repeated the bottom bar). The body waits for every list before it draws, so nothing pushes the page down later.
- API: `GET /api/hiring/me/files/` (their signed offer, handbook signatures and check-ins with PDF paths, plus the current handbook and whether they signed it) and `GET /api/hiring/me/offers/<id>/pdf/` (their own signed offer only). No migration.
- Tests: `apps/hiring/tests/test_my_files.py` (every kind, newest first, a new handbook version to sign, every PDF opens for its owner and for nobody else), `pages/home/HrTab.test.tsx` (Files order across time zones, to-sign first).

User-facing theme: **Home > Time clock replaces Today: the clock card with this week's hours and the overtime warning, then Mine (this pay period's shifts, pay so far and expected, fix a shift, time off) or Everyone (the week's schedule, read only) (Dash Update #137).**

> **For Bill:** Home > Time clock. On top: clock in, break and clock out, and beside it this week's hours against the 40 (or their overtime allowance), with one warning line: hours left, "clock out by 4:30" in the last hour, "clock out now" at the limit, and a heads-up when the schedule would push the week past it. It also shows the next shift. Below, a switch. **Mine:** the pay period with hours worked, pay so far and pay expected if they work the rest of their schedule (pay hidden until Show pay; family off payroll see hours only). Then every shift with a Fix button that sends you a time change request, and time off (unpaid) with each request's answer. **Everyone:** this week's schedule, the same grid you build on, read only. Staff no longer have a separate Schedule page; old links open Everyone. Building the week stays a manager tool in the HR workspace. The routine list and running a routine moved to the Routines tab.

### Added (server)
- `GET /api/hr/time-entries/my_period/` (`apps/hr/services/my_period.py`): the signed-in person's current pay period. Every day with what was scheduled and worked; each shift's latest fix request; days off; hours and pay so far (as `my_pay`); hours still ahead on the schedule (later days in full, today from now, nothing once clocked out today); expected pay with overtime split per Mon-Sun week; each week's worked, ahead and cleared hours; their schedule requests. User-facing theme: **Home > Profile rebuilt: my photo, my contact details, my work details and my next review, on one clean page (Dash Update #139).**

> **For Bill:** Home > Profile is new, phone first. At the top: your photo (tap it to take or pick one, or remove it), your name, job and department, EMP number, role and time here. Then **Contact** (phone and emergency contact are the person's to change with Edit, as before; name and email stay yours to change), **Work** (department, job, level, employment, pay rate, overtime allowance and start date, read only for staff; someone not on payroll sees "Not on payroll"), **Next review** (the next 30/60/90-day check-in, or the review date you set, whichever is first, with how far off it is) and **Sign-in** (password, register PIN, sign out on all devices). Hours, pay totals, shifts, messages and requests are not on it: those are the Time clock, Messages and HR tabs. In the staff window the photo is the same circle (you can set it there too, tap it), and **Employment** has a new **Next review** date that shows on their Profile. Your own photo also shows in the top bar. The /me page (Thrift+ Staff, HR > Me) is unchanged.

User-facing theme: **The HR workspace is for managers and the owner: one Requests inbox, the Schedule builder and Incidents on top (Dash Update #140, step 5 of the Home and HR plan).**

> **For Bill:** the HR workspace in the sidebar now opens on **Requests**: everything waiting on you in one list, the longest waiting first. Days off and new times, shift fixes (yours alone, as before), open incident reports and questions to HR. Type a reply and press Approve or Decline right on the row; a question gets Send, an incident gets Add note or Close, and Open the report shows the whole report. A name opens the staff window. **Answered** shows the last 30 days with who answered. The Requests link carries a red count. **Schedule** under it is the builder: the only place the week is changed. Everywhere else the week shows (HR > Schedule, Home), it is read-only, for managers too. **Incidents** lists the reports to follow up. Staff no longer see the HR workspace: their requests, the week and their profile are on Home. The HR page from Home (Requests, Me, Schedule, Incidents) no longer repeats the managers' pages; an old link to one of them opens the page in the workspace.

### Changed (HR workspace, Dash Update #140; `.ai/extended/home-and-hr-structure.md` decisions 4, 6 and 9)
- HR sidebar workspace: managers and the owner only (group `roles` Manager/Admin; each page keeps its own dial, so someone granted one HR page still finds it there). Order: Requests, Schedule, Incidents, Staff, Applicants, Interviews, Onboarding, Check-ins, Time & payroll (owner), Payroll deductions; under Setup: Permissions, Departments, Shifts, Jobs & careers page, Emails, HR documents (owner). The "My HR" items (Requests, Schedule, Profile) left the sidebar; HR > Requests is a Home page in the page registry.
- New **Requests** inbox, `/people/requests` (`pages/hr/inbox/HrInboxPage.tsx`, `useHrInbox.ts`), on the page kit (PageShell; the top bar names it; its table turns into cards on a phone). It reads the existing APIs, so no server change: schedule requests (answered by the schedule's managers), time-entry change requests (the owner, `hr.mod_requests:triage`), open incidents (the incidents API's `can_manage`), HR questions (`hr.questions:answer`). A person's own requests never show in their inbox. Waiting / Answered (last 30 days). The sidebar badge (`hooks/useNavBadgeCounts.ts`, item `hrInbox`) counts the waiting rows from the same queries; staff answer nothing, so nothing is fetched for them.
- **Schedule builder**: `SchedulePage` takes `build`; only `/people/schedule` passes it (kit page, no title in the body). Without it (HR > Schedule, any embed) the week is read-only, managers included. The `/hr/schedule` alias left the sidebar item. The week grid's tables are marked `eco-no-stack` (a desk view; the phone has its own day view).
- **Incidents** page, `/people/incidents` (`pages/hr/HrIncidentsPage.tsx`): the incidents list on the kit (`HrIncidents kit` drops the staff intro and the list heading). Staff still file and follow theirs at HR > Incidents.
- The split: the HR page (`/hr`, `pages/hr/HrPage.tsx`) keeps Requests, Me and Incidents for everyone; its People, Hiring, Onboarding and Time & payroll tabs (the same pages as the workspace) are gone, and their old addresses (`/hr/hiring/jobs` and so on, `MOVED`) open the page in the workspace.
- Texts stays out of HR: Bill made it owner only, on the Owner page (v2.302.0).
- Bundled with #137, #138 and #139 (one release): the HR page's Schedule tab went too (staff read the week on Time clock > Everyone, managers build it in HR > Schedule; `/hr/schedule` goes to `/people/schedule`, which sends staff to Everyone). `pages/people/MyOnboardingBanner.tsx` is removed (the onboarding banner is on Home > HR now). The Home band's row of tabs is hidden below 900 px (`FloorPageBand`): the phone tab bar at the bottom has the same tabs, and the row covered the tab's title.
- One inbox: HR > Requests for staff (`pages/hr/HrRequests.tsx`, `/hr`) no longer has the managers' "Waiting on you" block; whoever answers a kind gets a line there that opens the inbox. The **Sign-in** bar (#121, who is locked out) moved from that page to the top of the inbox (People at Manage).
