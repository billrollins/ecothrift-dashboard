feat(tars): rebuild the queue and bench around what can be read at a glance

Stage 5 of finalize_tars_app. Bill reviewed the studio on the floor and
rejected its shape: the scoreboard was bolted onto the Inbox lane, inline
alerts pushed the work around whenever state changed, and the bench had
grown past what anyone could take in at once.

Grading moves out of the studio. An item cannot reach a bench until its
grades are priced and only Ashley knows the prices, but that step lived on
Mike's screen where she had no reason to be. The queue is now one component
mounted both at /restoration/queue and on TARS Home, with no role gate, so
whoever is at a screen when the answer is known can record it. This exposed
a real defect: the existing patch endpoint accepted only queued jobs, so
anything already sent could not be edited at all. The new queue-details
endpoint stays open until the item is finished.

The bench loses the cockpit, state bar, lane list, work-bench table and
decision-session hook - about 1,950 lines - for a grade table. One row per
grade; aiming the clock at a row is the decision, so there is no separate
commit step to forget. Rates are read against a floor and a benchmark rather
than one pass/fail line.

Every inline Alert is gone. Standing conditions collect behind a header badge
and open in a drawer, and the no-layout-shift rule is recorded in
.ai/extended/frontend.md so it does not have to be relearned.
