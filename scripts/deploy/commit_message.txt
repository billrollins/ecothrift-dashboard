v2.334.0 — Black Friday: one price, the Black Friday site, Owner Testing tab

User-facing theme: **Black Friday: one price for everyone, the Black Friday site on phones and computers, and an Owner Testing tab**

> **For Bill:** Friday and Saturday every item rings up and scans at our tag less the ThriftBucks it would show on Saturday (at least 10% off), the same for guests and members, retail crossed out; phones get the Black Friday front page from Thursday, computers the Black Friday panel. Owner > Testing lets you link your phone and computer and see Mobile (Legacy, New, Black Friday) or Desktop (Black Friday) for 24 hours. Items marked sold by mistake still price and scan.

### Added
- **Black Friday price** (Dash Update #220, rulebook F5): `black_friday_price(item)` in `apps/pos/services/black_friday.py`, the one method: our tag less the ThriftBucks the item would show on Saturday (the nightly engine's `step` run forward night by night), never below the floor; a 10%-off minimum is one switch. AppSetting `pos.black_friday` `{show_from, start, end, bucks_on, ten_percent_min}` (store time; off when missing): the register charges from `start` to `end`, the website and scanners show it from `show_from`. `manage.py black_friday_prices` exports it for the ad (`--project`, `--json`, `--summary`). `Cart.recalculate` puts it on every sale line of an open sale as a dollar store sale (`CartLine.sale_off`, label `black_friday`, migration `pos/0053_black_friday`), before Thrift+; a Black Friday line collects no ThriftBucks and writes nothing to the ledger, so sales numbers count no Thrift+ revenue for it.
- **Build** for a member on Black Friday (`CartMember.builds`, migration `thriftplus/0035_black_friday`, `POST /api/thriftplus/register/build/`): pays the tag and keeps the ThriftBucks; the Thrift+ panel offers it beside the Black Friday price (the default).
- **Register line:** a Black Friday chip, the retail crossed out (only a retail the tag may print, new `retail` on the line) and the Black Friday price; no Sale savings row for it.
- **Receipt** (print server 1.10.1 unchanged): the item name carries "(Retail $X)" or "(Black Friday)" and the price paid; no ThriftBucks collected or missed and no "Join for" line on a Black Friday line.
- **Scanners** (the phone scanner and the current one): the scan card's price is the Black Friday price with no ThriftBucks (`black_friday: true`), labelled Black Friday; the trip shows its dollar total. A signed-out phone scans at once while it is on (session `black_friday`).
- **Website:** the window turns the phone site's Black Friday theme on by itself (`storefront.day` reads bf), its prices are the Black Friday price with no ThriftBucks, and the browser bar goes black. A computer keeps the desktop site. Home leads with the sale in the owner's words and one tap to scan; the guest's scanner shows one small join note that never blocks.

- **Black Friday takes over the front page** (owner 11-24): the first screen is the sale in his words and one button. Phones: Scan, on the phone site (`phone/pages/home/BlackFridayHero`; the header, search and bottom bar stay, the rest of home is below). Computers: Get directions, on the desktop site's own home (`components/BlackFridayPanel`, under the desktop header and menu; the visit block stays below). The server stamps the day on the desktop site's pages and the panel's first paint on its home (`templates/public/black_friday_shell.html`), so nothing swaps.
- **A desktop page is never a mobile page** (owner 11-24: "NEVER NEVER NEVER have the desktop app have Mobile elements or be Mobile view"): a computer is never sent to the phone site, Black Friday included. Written into `.ai/extended/phone-site.md` ("One website").
- **Frozen prices:** each item's Black Friday number is saved the first time it is worked out (`pos.BlackFridayPrice`, migration `pos/0054_black_friday_price`; `manage.py black_friday_prices --freeze` saves the whole floor at once), so the ad, website, scanners and register cannot drift apart as stock sells. A new tag works it out again.
- **Try it before Friday:** a training register charges the Black Friday price as soon as the setting is there.
- **Guest Wi-Fi:** the scan limit for phones that are not signed in is 3,000 a minute while Black Friday shows (one Wi-Fi is one address). Drinks stay at their price. The scanner phones use today wears the Black Friday look too.

- **Owner page, Testing tab** (owner 11-24: "testing settings so i can see ... how i want ... unselect and go to prod after 24 hours ... not accidently get stuck on a version no one sees"): `/owner/testing`. One pick for Mobile (Live, Legacy, New, Black Friday) and one for Desktop (Live, Black Friday; New is listed when the desktop layout exists); a pick runs 24 hours and then reads as Live by itself, and End now ends it at once. Only the owner's linked devices follow the picks: the tab's QR (a phone) or Link this computer sets the signed `eco_test` cookie once (and drops the old 30-day preview cookie). A linked device with a pick on shows a floating TEST badge with the version and its end time. Black Friday as a test shows for that request only (`black_friday.force`), so no customer and no shared list is touched. `apps/core/services/site_testing.py`, `GET/POST /api/core/owner/testing/`, AppSetting `owner.site_testing`.
