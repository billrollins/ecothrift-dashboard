v2.187.2 — Signing an offer starts onboarding; the owner's checklist, three pay levels and a red overtime flag

User-facing theme: **Onboarding runs by itself once an offer is signed, with the owner's checklist, three pay levels and an overtime flag (Bill, 2026-10-20).** Initiative: hiring_onboarding.

### Added
- **Signing an offer does the rest:** the new hire gets their Dash login, the onboarding checklist, the 30/60/90-day check-ins and the first-day email (once). If something blocks it (no email, or a Dash account already uses it), the offer still signs and the applicant's history says why; Create employee and Start onboarding still work. Practice runs never send anything.
- **Catch-up request:** "Start hires whose offer was already signed" (Dash → Requests) starts the hires who got stuck before this and can set overtime allowances.
- **Three pay levels:** Entry $15–17, Senior $18–19, Manager $20+ (a setting only the owner edits). The level shows next to pay on the offer, Create employee, Add employee and the employee page, with a warning (never a block) when pay is outside its range. The 90-day review sets the level. Existing employees got the level their pay falls in.
- **Over their hours:** a big red flag when someone's week goes past 40 plus their overtime allowance (none unless set): on the kiosk, their Hours & pay, People, and the morning brief, with a link to fix the time entry ("Forgot to clock out?").

### Changed
- **The onboarding checklist** follows the owner's list: handbook; I-9; schedule; 2 T-shirts; first-day email and orientation day; QuickBooks; added to WhatsApp; signed in to Dash; first payroll, no issues (about 3 weeks in); the 30, 60 and 90-day check-ins (they tick themselves when signed or skipped); then emergency contact, time clock, kiosk badge, first clock-in, team intro, training and the Nebraska report. An edited checklist keeps its items and gains the new ones; onboardings already in progress are unchanged.
