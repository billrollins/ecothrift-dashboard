Release: v2.112.0

- Inventory count (/inventory/count): a phone-first shelf count for a Bluetooth or
  USB scanner. Scans are queued on the phone and looked up in the background
  (beep = found, two beeps = warning, buzz and "back up N scans ago" = no item).
  The count freezes what is on the shelf when it starts. Managers get a shrink
  report and a CSV. New app apps/stocktake, migration stocktake/0001 (2 new tables).
- Thrift+ > Floor stock (Dash, superuser): a what-if calculator for stock already
  on the floor when Thrift+ starts. Replays the reward engine's rules, changes no
  price or setting. Endpoints: rewards/floor-plan/ and rewards/floor-plan/compare/.
- Optional AWS_LOCATION setting (S3 key prefix). Unset = today's behaviour.
- Repo standards: .ai/ in the house shape, scripts/ regrouped (dev, env, db,
  deploy), retired numbered deploy scripts.
