feat(tars): make the restoration queue a full-width row you edit in place

Bill's review of the queue: the rows used about two thirds of the page and
stacked their fields on top of each other, wasting the length, and every edit
went through a dialog when the whole point is filling in twenty items fast.

The row now runs the full width and reads left to right - what it is, a note,
where it goes, what it sells for at each grade, what is at stake, how long it
has waited, and the action. Every field in the middle is live. Focus selects
what is there, typing replaces it, Tab moves on and saves. There is no Save
button because leaving a field is the commit, and success is silent: a toast
per field would be unreadable when a whole queue is being filled in. The
details dialog is gone.

Queue, Bench and Holding are now three tabs over the same row, so an item does
not stop being editable the moment it reaches a bench - the person who knows
what something is worth is rarely the one holding it. Each list colours the
left edge of its rows and its own tab to match, so a glance tells you where
you are. Opens on Queue.

Restoration queue now leads the Restoration nav group, ahead of TARS.
