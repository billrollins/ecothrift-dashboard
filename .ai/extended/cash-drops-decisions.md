# Cash Drops: decisions (Bill, 2026-11-04, from the #67 session)

A redesign of how cash moves through the day. Its own Dash Update (not part of #67). Q&A rows: `.ai/reference/owner-qa-log.md` (11-04, Cash Drops).

## The day
1. **Open:** the opener does the first count and opens the drawer.
2. **Cover:** someone watching the register puts in their PIN and checks people out. They count the drawer or at least accept responsibility for it. When the cashier comes back, they put in their PIN, see the cash transactions and the new count (changed denominations highlighted), and accept the drawer.
3. **Handoff (mid-shift):** a drawer count at every handoff; coins can be skipped.
4. **Close:** the closer counts dollars and coins and confirms the final count.
5. **Supplemental drawer exchange:** first, so the drawer holds its starting cash.
6. **Cash drop:** all the rest goes in an envelope into the Cash Drop box. Then it starts over the next day.

## Supplemental drawer
- Exchanges only with the safe and always keeps the same total (about $300 exactly, or whatever gives the best change). $10 of quarters costs a $10 bill.

## Bank deposit (owner or someone he names)
- Collect every cash drop; check each envelope's total and bills against what was expected.
- Small bills stay in the safe; $20s and $10s go to the bank.
- Record the deposit and the safe transaction. Make change for the supplemental drawer or the safe when needed.
