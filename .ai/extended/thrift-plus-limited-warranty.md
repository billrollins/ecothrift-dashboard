# Thrift+ Limited Warranty (research review and the text to use)

**Source:** "Thrift+ Limited Warranty Review", Eco-Thrift LLC, Oct 1, 2026, pasted by the owner on 2026-10-01. Short-form AI research for attorney review, not legal advice. It answers the research request written the same day. It builds on [`thrift-plus-legal-memo.md`](thrift-plus-legal-memo.md) (Q12), which the owner's CPA and attorney accepted. **The owner chose defect-only returns ("main function"), done as a labeled Limited Warranty.** Load this file before touching returns, Poster C, receipts, AS IS signs or the terms.

## What this changes (digest, for the coder)

1. **The member return is a "Thrift+ Limited Warranty".** Use the poster text below, in one document. The receipt only points to it. Poster C in the launch kit is replaced by it.
2. **AS IS notices before the sale are the biggest exposure.** Needed: a sign at every register and the entrance, tag stamps on as-is / for-parts / untested items, signs in the clothing, 18+ and crossbow areas, and receipt lines. An excluded item with no AS IS notice still carries a full implied warranty.
3. **Receipts:** on member receipts each line is marked **LW** (covered) or **AS IS**, with the two footer lines below, and the warranty's version date. Guest receipts carry the all-AS-IS line. Needs register and print-server work: the register must know which items are excluded.
4. **The remedy:** store credit for 95% of the price paid **plus the sales tax on that amount** (not "before tax"), at no charge, as the sole and exclusive remedy. A fallback: if store credit can't be used, refund the same amount in cash. Store credit never expires and has no fees. The returns code today credits 95% before tax and does not credit the tax.
5. **Claims:** card or account phone number (a lost card is fine; the sales record proves the sale). Staff check the main function. A member may ask a manager to review a denial. No "in our sole discretion".
6. **DECISION, 3 days or 7:** the 3-day limit on implied warranties is "the most attackable term in the program". 7 days costs little and cuts the risk a lot. If 3 stays, it is a known risk.
7. **DECISION, the $10 monthly "cover":** saying rewards "cover the card" reads like a monthly charge taken from rewards, which undercuts "free" and invites a service-contract argument. In the system nothing is taken from the member's money: the first $10 of rewards each month is simply not given. The fix is at least the wording (for example "rewards start after your first $10 of rewards each month"), and never "covers your card".
8. **Fill-ins the poster needs:** the store's street address, zip and phone, and a version date. Date each version, keep old ones, print the version date on receipts.
9. **Guest line stays off the warranty poster**; it lives on the AS IS sign, which names the member exception.
10. Not insurance and not a service contract while membership is free and nothing extra is charged.

## Answers

### A. Is it a warranty at all
1. **Written warranty?** Yes. A written promise to "refund, repair, replace, or take other remedial action" if a product fails a stated standard is a written warranty. Store credit counts as "other remedial action", and the 3-day period supplies the time limit. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2301(6)(B); 16 CFR 700.3(a). Confidence: High.
2. **Is 95% store credit a real remedy?** Yes. The Act sets minimum remedy standards only for Full warranties. A problem only if the credit can't actually be used (Q17). Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2304. Confidence: High.
3. **Can wording avoid warranty status?** No. Substance controls. The FTC exempts only returns allowed "for any reason" and unconditional satisfaction guarantees. A defect-based return called a "return policy" is still a warranty, a noncompliant one, and the disclaimers would fail. The labeled Limited Warranty is the safer route. Basis: 16 CFR 700.3(b), 700.5. Confidence: High.

### B. Required form and content
4. **Title.** "Limited Warranty" is required for products over $10, as a caption or prominent title separate from the text. "Thrift+ Limited Warranty" works. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2303(a); 16 CFR 700.6. Confidence: High.
5. **Required contents for items over $15** (16 CFR 701.3(a)), against the first draft:

| 701.3(a) item | First draft |
|---|---|
| (1) Who is covered, if limited to the original buyer | Partly. Say "the member who bought it." |
| (2) What is covered and excluded | Partly. Define "main function" (Q21). |
| (3) What you will do, at whose expense, what you won't pay for | Mostly. Add "no charge", fix the tax wording (Q20). |
| (4) When coverage starts and how long it lasts | Partly. Say how the 3 days are counted. |
| (5) Step-by-step claim procedure, with the warrantor's name and address or phone | Missing |
| (6) Informal dispute mechanism | Not needed (Q23) |
| (7) Implied warranty limit plus the required sentence | Present |
| (8) Exclusion of incidental or consequential damages plus the required sentence | Missing (needed only if excluded; Q18) |
| (9) The "specific legal rights" sentence | Present |

   Required sentences, verbatim:
   - (7): "Some States do not allow limitations on how long an implied warranty lasts, so the above limitation may not apply to you."
   - (8): "Some States do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so the above limitation or exclusion may not apply to you."
   - (9): "This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights which vary from State to State."

   (7) and (8) may be combined into one sentence. Basis: 16 CFR 701.2, 701.3(a) and (b). Confidence: High.
6. **Items $15 or less.** The disclosure rules (Part 701) and pre-sale rules (Part 702) don't apply. The "Limited Warranty" title applies above $10. The ban on disclaiming implied warranties applies at any price once a written warranty is given. One document for every price is simplest. Basis: 16 CFR 701.2, 702.1; 15 U.S.C. §§ 2303(a), 2308. Confidence: High.
7. **Name, address, start date, claim steps.** Yes. Name Eco-Thrift LLC with a mailing address or phone, and give step-by-step claim instructions. Spell out how the 3 days are counted. Basis: 16 CFR 701.3(a)(4), (5). Confidence: High.
8. **Single document; plain language.** All nine items in one document: the poster. The receipt can only point to it. In practice: large high-contrast type, short sentences, no fine print, the title set apart. Basis: 16 CFR 701.3(a). Confidence: High.

### C. Making it available before the sale
9. **Seller-warrantor duties.** For items over $15, either show the text close to each warranted product, or furnish it on request before sale with prominent signs saying it's available. With store-wide coverage use the second: the poster at each register, copies at the counter, and floor signs saying "Ask for the Thrift+ Limited Warranty." The receipt doesn't count; it arrives after the sale. Basis: 16 CFR 702.3(a). Confidence: Medium-high. Verify: whether a register poster alone also satisfies "close proximity".
10. **App and website.** Helpful, not required, not a substitute for in-store availability. Basis: 16 CFR 702.3(a), (b)(2). Confidence: Medium.

### D. Limiting and disclaiming implied warranties
11. **Limiting implied warranties to 3 days.** Allowed in principle, but the most attackable term in the program. The limit must be "of reasonable duration" and "conscionable"; no case sets a floor for used goods. It fails if a typical defect can't show up within 3 days, or if a court sees 3 days as built to defeat claims. Going to 7 days would cost little and cut the risk a lot. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2308(b). Confidence: Low to medium. Verify: whether counsel is comfortable with 3 days.
12. **Required language and placement.** On the face of the warranty (the poster), in clear and unmistakable words, prominently displayed; bold type near the remedy. Suggested: "Any implied warranty, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose, lasts only 3 days from the day of sale." Then the required sentence. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2308(b); 16 CFR 701.3(a)(7). Confidence: High.
13. **Member purchases of excluded items.** They can be fully disclaimed, because no written warranty was given on those products. But exclusion alone doesn't disclaim: without an AS IS notice, an excluded item still carries the full implied warranty. Every excluded category needs its own AS IS notice before the sale: tag stamps for as-is, for-parts and untested goods, and signs for clothing, 18+ items and crossbows. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2308(a); Neb. U.C.C. § 2-316(3)(a). Confidence: Medium.
14. **AS IS for guests.** "As is" excludes all implied warranties. It must be conspicuous and seen before payment. A large sign at each register is likely enough; add the entrance and the receipt for backup. A receipt alone is too late. Basis: Neb. U.C.C. § 2-316(3)(a); § 1-201(b)(10). Confidence: Medium-high.
15. **Same item, AS IS for guests and warranty for members.** Fine. Each sale is its own contract. Have the AS IS sign name the exception, and keep the guest line off the warranty poster. Confidence: Medium-high.
16. **Used Car Rule and other used-goods rules.** The Used Car Rule covers only vehicle dealers. No general federal used-goods warranty rule. Nebraska bars passing off used goods as new. Basis: 16 CFR Part 455; Neb. Rev. Stat. § 87-302(a)(7). Confidence: High. Verify: whether Omaha's secondhand-dealer ordinance applies to the inventory sources (a licensing question).

### E. The remedy and its limits
17. **Store credit as the exclusive remedy.** Yes, if the text says so; "sole and exclusive remedy" removes doubt. It fails of its essential purpose when the member can't get the benefit (the store closes, the member is banned, staff refuse valid claims). Add a fallback: if store credit can't be used, refund 95% in cash. Basis: Neb. U.C.C. § 2-719(1)(b), (2). Confidence: Medium-high.
18. **Excluding incidental and consequential damages.** Yes, unless unconscionable; carve out personal injury. Wording: "We are not responsible for incidental or consequential damages, such as damage to other property or travel costs. This does not limit claims for personal injury." Then the combined sentence. Basis: Neb. U.C.C. § 2-719(3); 16 CFR 701.3(a)(8), (b). Confidence: High.
19. **Keeping 5%.** Lawful for a Limited warranty. Describe it as the amount of the credit ("store credit for 95% of what you paid"), not as a restocking charge. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2304. Confidence: Medium-high.
20. **Sales tax on the returned amount.** Credit the tax on the 95%: on a $100 item at 7%, $95 plus $6.65. Say "plus the sales tax on that amount"; "before tax" reads as if tax is kept. Basis: Neb. Reg-1-025.01; 16 CFR 701.3(a)(3). Confidence: High.

### F. Who decides, and disputes
21. **"Main function does not work."** Workable, but define it with examples: "It does the main thing it's made for: a lamp lights, a blender blends, a toaster heats." Let a member ask a manager to review a denial. No "in our sole discretion". Basis: 16 CFR 701.3(a)(2). Confidence: Medium.
22. **Requiring the card and the store's records.** Yes, if the poster says so. Accept a phone-number lookup when the card is lost. Basis: 16 CFR 701.3(a)(1), (5). Confidence: High.
23. **Informal dispute procedure.** Optional. Skip it and leave it off the poster. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2310(a)(3); 16 CFR 703.1. Confidence: High.
24. **Exposure.** A member can sue in state court; Nebraska small claims takes cases up to $7,500 (July 1, 2025). A consumer who wins may recover costs and attorney fees, the real multiplier on a $20 item. Federal court needs $50,000 in total or a class with 100 named plaintiffs; a class suit must first give a chance to cure. The FTC can enforce; Nebraska's consumer protection acts add AG penalties. The realistic risk is a store-wide defect, such as void disclaimers, across many sales. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2310(b), (d), (e); Neb. Rev. Stat. § 25-2802. Confidence: High.

### G. Is it insurance or a service contract
25. **Service contract or insurance?** Neither, as long as it's free. A warranty is part of the bargain at the sale with no charge beyond the price; a service contract costs extra or is bought later. Basis: 15 U.S.C. § 2301(8); 16 CFR 700.11. Confidence: Medium-high.
26. **The $10 monthly "cover".** The soft spot. Saying the first $10 of rewards each month is "applied as a cover for the card" means the member pays $10 a month in rewards to stay a member. That cuts against "free", gives a service-contract or insurance argument something to grab, and looks like a monthly fee taken from a reward balance. The fix: take nothing for membership. If the $10 is a perk, describe it as a monthly reward given to the member. Say on the poster: "Included with covered purchases at no extra charge." Basis: 16 CFR 700.11; 16 CFR 251.1. Confidence: Medium. Depends on: what the cover actually does in the system. If nothing is ever taken from the member, only the wording needs fixing.
27. **Nebraska service contract law.** Only a motor vehicle service contract statute was found. Basis: Neb. Rev. Stat. § 44-3522. Confidence: Medium. Verify: that no general service contract act exists.

### H. Changes over time
28. **Changing or ending it.** Yes, for future sales; post the new version before it takes effect. Each sale keeps the warranty in force on its sale date. Date each version, keep old ones, and print the version date on receipts. Basis: 16 CFR 700.11. Confidence: High.

## The text to use

Fill in the bracketed items. The text keeps 3 days; if it moves to 7 (Q11), change every "3" and the example.

### Poster (13 x 19 in)

```text
THRIFT+ LIMITED WARRANTY
Eco-Thrift LLC · [street address], Omaha, NE [zip] · [phone]

Who is covered
The Thrift+ member who bought the item with their card.

What is covered
Items whose main function does not work.
Main function means the main thing it is made for:
a lamp lights, a blender blends, a toaster heats.

Not covered
Items sold as-is, for parts, or untested.
Clothing and soft goods. 18+ items. Crossbows.
Scratches, wear, or missing small parts.
Change of mind.

How long
3 days after the day of sale.
Bought Monday? Bring it back by Thursday.
If we are closed on day 3, the next day we are open counts.

How to make a claim
1. Bring the item to any register within 3 days.
2. Show your Thrift+ card or give your account phone number.
3. Staff check that the main function does not work.
4. If we say no, you may ask a manager to review.

What we will do
At no charge to you, we give store credit for 95% of the
price you paid, plus the sales tax on that amount.
This store credit is your sole and exclusive remedy.
We do not repair, replace, or refund cash, except:
if we cannot provide usable store credit, we will refund
the same amount in cash.
Store credit never expires and has no fees.

Limits
ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND
FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, LASTS ONLY 3 DAYS
FROM THE DAY OF SALE.
We are not responsible for incidental or consequential
damages, such as damage to other property or travel costs.
This does not limit claims for personal injury.

Some States do not allow limitations on how long an implied
warranty lasts, or the exclusion or limitation of incidental
or consequential damages, so the above limitations or
exclusions may not apply to you.

This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may
also have other rights which vary from State to State.

Included with covered member purchases at no extra charge.
Version [date]
```

## Most likely to be challenged (fix in this order)

1. **AS IS notices not shown before the sale.** The biggest exposure. If guests and excluded items don't see a conspicuous AS IS before paying, every one of those sales carries a full implied warranty, with up to 4 years to sue. Fix first: register and entrance signs, tag stamps, receipt lines (Q13, Q14; Neb. U.C.C. §§ 2-316, 2-725).
2. **The 3-day implied warranty limit.** It must be a "reasonable duration" and conscionable; 3 days for used goods is untested. If it fails, member items carry implied warranties of normal length. Fix: move to 7 days, or accept the risk knowingly (Q11).
3. **Incomplete warranty text.** The first draft lacked the warrantor's name and contact, claim steps, a clear "exclusive" remedy and a definition of "main function", and its tax wording was wrong. Fix: use the poster above (Q5, Q17, Q20, Q21).

Also fix the $10 "cover" wording before launch, so membership is plainly free (Q26).
