# Thrift+ rulebook

**The one place for every Thrift+ rule.** Form 5 is chosen: rewards first, as store credit. Code follows this file. Owners decide on the rulebook page (https://claude.ai/artifact/GpgB6SUsKkiKkNBRJj31W4); answers are folded in here. Items marked "CPA / attorney" carry our answer and wait for their sign-off. Older notes (the 10-01 list, the legal memo, the warranty review, the launch kit) lose wherever they differ.

## The program in one look

*Form 5: rewards first, as store credit. Everything below follows from it.*

- **F1. Form 5: rewards first** (Decided). Every reward is earned in full and kept on the account as Rewards, a loyalty reward the store gives (not bought), good for 30 days. At checkout the member can use today's rewards now at 80%, or save them in full. Saving is the default. Compared with the other forms on https://claude.ai/artifact/AnH8RuYAKANXGoqBq1crxf.
  - Customer words: "Every find earns Rewards. Save them in full for 30 days, or use 80% today."
  - Source: Your picks 10-08. Built: No (the build follows these rules).
- **F2. Warranty refunds go back as store credit** (Decided). 80% of what they spent, tax included (the same 80% as using rewards today), goes back as credit on the same Thrift+ balance, with a cash refund if the credit can't be used (for example the store closes or the membership ends). The money part comes back as refund credit, which never expires; see W3.
  - Source: Your picks 10-08. Built: No.
- **F3. The whole trip, worked** (Decided). Tag $80, rewards $35, 7% tax, cover already filled, bought Oct 20. Save it: pay $85.60, get $35 credit, use by Nov 19. Use it today: $28 off (80%), pay $55.64. Next trip, a $50 item with that $35 credit: $15 taxed = $16.05. Early in a month with $10 of cover left: $10 covers the card, then save $25 or use $20 today.
  - Source: Worked 10-08. Built: n/a.

## The monthly $10 cover

*Always on. It comes out of rewards first.*

- **C1. How the cover works** (Decided). The first $10 of rewards each calendar month covers the card. It comes out of the full-value rewards first, before the use-or-save choice. It resets on the 1st and never rolls over; a month with less than $10 of rewards simply covers less. Active staff are exempt while your staff switch is on.
  - Customer words: "Free to get your card. Your first $10 of rewards each month covers it, so you never pay anything out of pocket."
  - Source: Design 09-25; your messages 10-08. Built: Yes (cover built; it already comes first).
- **C2. Always a cover** (Decided). Every form keeps the $10 monthly cover.
  - Source: Your message 10-08. Built: Yes.
- **C3. Saying "free"** (Decided). "Free to get your card" always comes with the cover line next to it (C1). Never "free forever", and nothing that promises replacement cards are free.
  - Source: Your message 10-08; legal review. Built: n/a.
- **C4. Cover and taxes** (Decided). Rewards that go to the cover don't lower the price: the item is taxed at the tag.
  - Source: Follows C1. Built: Yes.

## Joining

*Who can join and what a card needs before it works.*

- **J1. Who can join** (Decided). 18 or older. Up to 2 adults per membership; both must be there to add the second, and the primary approves. Both share one Thrift+ credit balance.
  - Source: 10-08; design 09-25. Built: Partly (the age rule is not enforced yet).
- **J2. What a card needs before it works** (Decided). A photo, name and phone, an ID check, and a signature. Until all four are done the card is "not ready": it earns and uses nothing.
  - Source: Meeting 10-07. Built: No (due 10-12).
- **J3. The ID check** (Decided). Staff look at a photo ID for the name, the age and the face. Nothing from the ID is scanned, copied or kept: only "ID checked", by whom, when, and the 18+ flag.
  - Customer words: "We check your ID for age and keep nothing from it."
  - Source: 10-01; meeting 10-07. Built: Partly.
- **J4. The photo** (Decided). Taken with the register's webcam. Used only so staff can confirm it is you: never marketing, never shared, never face recognition. Deleted 24 months after the last purchase, or within 30 days of a request.
  - Customer words: "Your photo protects your card balance."
  - Source: 10-01; 10-08. Built: Partly (webcam view due 10-12).
- **J5. The signature** (Decided). Signed on the register's touch screen: a deliberate act that covers the photo release and the written terms, which make no cash value, expiry, final sale and the ID rules binding.
  - Customer words: "I agree to the Thrift+ terms and photo release. I can read them at ecothrift.us/thriftplus."

- **J6. Staff memberships** (Decided). Free for active staff (no cover) while your staff switch is on.

- **J7. 18+ items** (Decided). Membership never replaces the ID check: ID every time.

At the register they show a driver's license or other photo ID and sign the screen; staff check the ID against the photo and scan a card. Until then the sign-up is "not ready" (J2).

## Rewards and prices

*What members earn, and how it moves.*

- **P1. Guests and members** (Decided). Guests pay the tag. Members earn rewards on almost everything. Not earning: consignment items, an item's first 7 days on the floor, and back stock not yet seen on the floor.
  - Customer words: "Rewards on almost everything."
  - Source: Design; 10-08. Built: Yes.
- **P2. The word members see** (Decided). "Rewards" everywhere members look. "Member price" only as a plain description (the tag minus rewards used today).
  - Source: Your message 10-08. Built: No (screens say "Member Price" today).
- **P3. How rewards grow** (Decided). After a 7-day wait, rewards grow by 0.75% of the tag a day, up to 90% of the tag (a member never pays less than 10% of the tag when using today). Changes happen overnight; rewards never shrink except on a retag.
  - Source: 10-08; 10-01. Built: No (engine uses tag ÷ 90 a day; due 10-13).
- **P4. Launch jump start** (Decided). Stock on the floor at launch counts as half its real age, at most 45 days.
  - Source: 10-08. Built: No.
- **P5. Back stock** (Decided). Not scanned in the last inventory and not processed since: no rewards. Once scanned on the floor it matches its look-alikes (day 1 if none).
  - Source: 10-08. Built: No.
- **P6. Pacing families** (Decided). Families pace by the smooth rule (aim: starting count ÷ 90 a day; grow up to 0.75% a day in proportion to how far behind). You can set a family's own target or pause it; a daily review shows each family and why it moved.
  - Source: 10-08. Built: Partly.
- **P7. Back stock and the floor** (Decided). Floor and back stock counts per product; a page to mark items moved to the floor; suggestions for moves either way.
  - Source: 10-08. Built: No.

## Thrift+ credit

*Saving rewards, refunds, and spending them.*

- **B1. Use today or save** (Decided). Rewards are always earned in full. The register asks once per trip: use today's rewards now at 80%, or save them in full on the account. Saving is the default; one choice covers the whole trip (no splitting); it is logged. 80% is rounded to the cent in the member's favor. Not asked when Thrift+ credit covers the total (B3).
  - Customer words: "Use today: $28 off. Or save $35 for 30 days."
  - Source: Your picks 10-08. Built: No.
- **B2. Two balances: Rewards and Thrift+ credit** (Decided). Saved rewards are shown as "Rewards", each amount with its use-by date. Only the money part of warranty refunds is "Thrift+ credit" (never expires). They are kept apart, and named apart, because the memo warns that "credit" reads like a payment for sales tax, while a reward is a discount; expiry, cash and state reporting differ too.
  - Customer words: "Your Rewards: $35.00, use by Nov 19 · Thrift+ credit: $12.20"
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q16). Built: No.
- **B3. Spending: Thrift+ credit always first** (Decided). Thrift+ credit (refund money) is always used first, automatically, up to the whole total, as a payment after tax. Rewards apply only to what is left: first any saved Rewards the member chooses to use (soonest use-by date first), then today's rewards at 80%. Because rewards come off before tax, the register works out the rewards so that the Thrift+ credit is spent in full first. If the Thrift+ credit covers the whole total, no rewards are used that trip: today's rewards are saved in full and saved Rewards stay saved. Rewards can't pay for consignment items; Thrift+ credit can. Items bought with credit or Rewards still earn rewards.
  - Customer words: "Thrift+ credit is always used first."
  - Source: Your rule 10-08. Built: No.
- **B14. Credit first, worked** (Decided). $80 item, 7% tax. With $100 of Thrift+ credit: the credit pays $85.60, no rewards are used, and today's $35 is saved. With $50 of Thrift+ credit and $35 of saved Rewards: $33.27 of Rewards bring the total to $50.00, which the credit pays in full; $1.73 of Rewards stay saved, and nothing is paid in cash.
  - Source: Worked 10-08. Built: n/a.
- **B4. No cap** (Decided). No limit on saved rewards: the 30-day expiry already keeps reward credit small.
  - Source: Follows your 30 days, 10-08. Built: n/a.
- **B5. Expiry, fees and cash** (Decided). Rewards expire 30 days after the receipt they were earned on. Thrift+ credit (refunds) never expires: it is money the member paid. No fees. Rewards have no cash value; Thrift+ credit is not redeemable for cash, except the warranty's cash fallback and where the law requires.
  - Customer words: "Saved $35.00 in Rewards. Use by Nov 19. Rewards have no cash value."
  - Source: Your picks 10-08 (replaces "never expires"). Built: No.
- **B13. Seeing it, and a reminder** (Decided). My account lists each saved amount with its use-by date, soonest first, and the refund credit apart. Members with an email get one reminder 5 days before rewards expire (after launch).
  - Source: Your picks 10-08; reminder by Claude. Built: No.
- **B6. Names** (Decided). "Rewards" for saved rewards; "Thrift+ credit" only for refund money. Never "gift card", "store credit" or "Thrift+ Cash" to members.
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q16). Built: No.
- **B7. Lost cards, two adults, no transfers** (Decided). One account per membership, shared by both adults. A lost card is stopped and a new card links to the same account. Rewards and credit can't be transferred to anyone else.
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q16: non-transferable). Built: Yes.
- **B8. Selling gift cards** (Decided). Not now. Nothing is sold as stored value, which keeps the federal gift card rules out entirely.
  - Source: Claude, at your word 10-08. Built: n/a.
- **B9. Wind-down safeguards** (Decided). Reward credit lasts 30 days, so what is owed stays small; cash equal to unspent refund credit sits in a separate account; the terms can end rewards going forward (B10); Dash shows both totals owed every day.
  - Source: Your question 10-08. Built: Partly (Overview shows owed).
- **B10. Ending or changing the program** (Decided). Changes apply going forward only, announced in store and online with notice. Rewards already earned stay usable until their date; Thrift+ credit is always honored or paid in cash. A membership ended for misuse loses its unused Rewards; its Thrift+ credit is still honored.
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q7: going forward, honor what is earned). Built: No.
- **B11. Unclaimed property** (Decided). Free rewards are not in Nebraska's unclaimed property act, so Rewards are not reported. Thrift+ credit from refunds unused for 3 years is reported to the State Treasurer as a credit memo. Optional: one call to the Treasurer's Unclaimed Property Division to confirm.
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q13). Built: No.
- **B12. Disclosures that make the 30 days work** (Decided). For rewards that expire, the memo's condition: the word "Reward" and the expiry rule printed on the card itself, and each date on receipts and in the app. Card line: "Thrift+ Rewards: use within 30 days of earning. No cash value." No gift cards are sold.
  - Customer words: "Thrift+ Rewards: use within 30 days of earning. No cash value."
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q13). Built: No (the card design and receipts change).

## Limited Warranty and returns

*What members can bring back, and what they get.*

- **W1. What it covers** (Decided). "Thrift+ Limited Warranty", for members: an item whose main function doesn't work (a lamp lights, a blender blends). Untested items are covered.
  - Customer words: "We can't test most items. This warranty covers them anyway."
  - Source: 10-01; 10-07. Built: Partly.
- **W2. How long** (Decided). 7 days, counted after the day of sale; a closed day 7 moves to the next open day.
  - Source: 10-07; 10-01. Built: No (built for 3).
- **W3. What they get back** (Decided). 80% of everything they spent on the item, tax included, in the form they spent it: 80% of the money (and Thrift+ credit) as Thrift+ credit, which never expires, with a cash fallback; 80% of any Rewards used, as Rewards with a fresh 30 days.
  - Customer words: "80% of what you spent, tax included, back on your account."
  - Source: Your picks 10-08 (80% for parity). Built: No (built as 95%).
- **W4. Rewards the returned item earned** (Decided). They are taken back: first from what the item put into this month's cover, then from unspent reward credit. If that credit was already spent, the refund is reduced by it; if it already expired, nothing is taken.
  - Source: Built rule; 10-08. Built: Partly (the reversal is built).
- **W5. Worked** (Decided). Lamp, $80 tag, $35 rewards. Saved and paid $85.60: refund credit $68.48, and the $35 reward credit it earned is taken back. Used today and paid $55.64: refund credit $44.51.
  - Source: Worked 10-08. Built: n/a.
- **W6. Not covered** (Decided). Items marked NO THRIFT+ WARRANTY (sold as-is or for parts), clothing and soft goods, 18+ items, crossbows, and items under $5. Never covered: scratches, wear, missing small parts, a change of mind.
  - Source: 10-01; 10-08 (under $5). Built: Partly.
- **W7. Making a claim** (Decided). Bring the item with the card or phone number; staff check the main function; a manager reviews any no.
  - Source: 10-01. Built: Partly.
- **W8. Where the full text lives** (Decided). Printed at every register and at ecothrift.us/thriftplus/warranty, version-dated, old versions kept. Eco-Thrift LLC, 8425 West Center Road, Omaha, NE 68124, (402) 881-9861.
  - Source: 10-01; 10-08. Built: No (page due 10-12).
- **W9. Guests** (Decided). Everything is sold AS IS. All sales final.
  - Customer words: "All sales final. Sold AS IS."
  - Source: Design. Built: Yes.

## Sales tax and the books

*What is taxed, and when.*

- **T1. Rewards used today** (Decided). The 80% comes off before tax: the item is taxed on the lower price.
  - Source: Follows F1. Built: No.
- **T2. Saving rewards** (Decided). The item is taxed at the tag; creating reward credit is not a sale.
  - Source: Follows F1. Built: No.
- **T3. Spending Rewards** (Decided). A discount on that later sale: tax is on the price after Rewards. Recorded as a discount line, never as a tender, so the books show it as a reward.
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q16). Built: No.
- **T4. Refund credit** (Decided). The refund includes 80% of the tax, which the store takes back on its return. Spending refund credit later is a payment: that sale is taxed in full.
  - Source: Follows F2. Built: No.
- **T5. The register change** (Decided). Go: the register taxes by these rules, as part of the form 5 build.
  - Source: Claude, at your word 10-08. Built: No.
- **T6. The books** (Decided). Unused Rewards and Thrift+ credit are liabilities until spent or expired (ASC 606 breakage). Records per sale: tag, each discount by type (rewards used, cover), net taxable price, tax, each tender; per member, a ledger of rewards earned, used and expired; a monthly match of the register totals to each Form 10.
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q14, Q19). Built: No.
