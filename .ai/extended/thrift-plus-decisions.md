## The program in one look

*Form 5: rewards first, as store credit. Everything below follows from it.*

- **F1. Form 5: ThriftBucks first** (Decided; changed 10-12). Every ThriftBuck is collected in full and kept on the account: a loyalty reward the store gives (not bought). Keeping them is the Default: usable from the next day for 30 days, at full value. A member who tells the cashier **redeems that trip's ThriftBucks instantly** instead, at **50% of their value**, off today's price (B1). There is no cover (C1): what an instant redemption leaves, and ThriftBucks that expire unused, pay for Thrift+ (F4). Compared with the other forms on https://claude.ai/artifact/AnH8RuYAKANXGoqBq1crxf.
  - Customer words: "Use your ThriftBucks next visit at full value, or redeem them instantly at 50%."
  - Why 50% (owner 10-12): the larger amount must be the one members count as the real reward; "half today or all of it next visit" brings them back, and an instant redemption pays for the program. Raising it later is good news; cutting it later would read as a takeaway.
  - Source: Your picks 10-08; signed 10-09; owner 10-12 (50% replaces 80%; "the noun is always ThriftBucks", instant redemption is the verb). Built: No (80% and "InstantBucks" are built).
- **F2. Return refunds go back as Thrift+ credit** (Decided; changed 10-12). 80% of what they spent, tax included, goes back as credit on the same Thrift+ balance, never back the way you paid (owner 10-12: fewer refunds to payment cards and cancellations), with a cash refund only if the credit can't be used (for example the store closes or the membership ends). Refund credit never expires; see W3.
  - Source: Your picks 10-08; owner 10-12. Built: No.
- **F3. The whole trip, worked** (Decided; redone 10-12). Tag $80, 35 ThriftBucks, 7% tax, bought Oct 20. Kept (Default): pay $85.60, 35 ThriftBucks usable Oct 21 to Nov 19. Redeemed instantly: $17.50 off (50%), pay $66.88. Next trip, a $50 item with those 35 ThriftBucks: $15 taxed = $16.05.
  - Source: Worked 10-08; redone 10-12 (no cover, 50%). Built: n/a.
- **F4. The books per item** (Decided 10-12, owner). For a truck's margin, an item is booked at its price minus the ThriftBucks it collected, whichever way the member takes them. What an instant redemption leaves above that, and ThriftBucks that expire unused, are Thrift+ income. Example, $100 price with 50 ThriftBucks: the truck books $50 every time; kept and spent later, Thrift+ $0; kept and expired, Thrift+ $50; redeemed instantly, the member pays $75 and Thrift+ gets $25. ## ThriftBucks and prices

*What members collect, and how it moves.*

- **P1. Non-members and members** (Decided; word changed 10-15). Non-members pay the tag. Members collect ThriftBucks on almost everything. Not collecting: an item's first 7 days on the floor, and back stock not yet seen on the floor.
  - Customer words: "ThriftBucks on most items." (the terms say "most items" and list the exceptions)
  - Source: Design; 10-08. Built: Yes.
- **P2. The word members see** (Decided; changed 10-10). "ThriftBucks" everywhere members look (V8). "Member price" only as a plain description (the tag minus ThriftBucks used today).
  - Source: Your message 10-08; V8 10-10. Built: Yes (V8).
- **P3. How rewards grow** (Decided). After a 7-day wait, rewards grow by 0.75% of the tag a day, up to 95% of the tag. There is no extra for keeping them: kept at full value is the normal way, and an instant redemption is 50% of it (B1). So the most off today is 47.5% of the tag. Changes happen overnight; ThriftBucks never shrink except on a retag. Customer words: ThriftBucks grow as an item sits. The daily rate is never printed (P8). The cap may be printed ("up to 95% of the tag", owner 10-09), and as a worked example ("up to $19" on a $20 tag).
  - Customer words: "ThriftBucks grow as an item sits."
  - Source: 10-08; 10-01; owner 10-09 (95% replaces 90%, because saving no longer earns 5% more); 10-12 (instant at 50%). Built: Yes (v2.163.0, 10-13: the engine runs on the Calculator's saved setting; the owner's start: 0.75% a day after 7 days, never below 5% of the tag; the owner clicks Save).
- **P4. Launch jump start** (Decided). Stock on the floor at launch starts at 30% of its real age (owner 10-13; was one third), at most 45 days, then ages one day per day. The 7-day wait (P3) still applies: an item 9 days old at launch starts at day 3 and earns nothing for 4 more days.
  - Source: 10-08; signed 10-09 (one third, replaces half); owner 10-13 (30%). Built: Yes (v2.163.0, from the Calculator's saved setting).
- **P5. Back stock** (Decided). Days on the floor don't grow while an item is in back stock. Moving an item to back stock resets its age to 0; the old age stays in its history, so the owner can resume it. Back stock not seen on the floor earns nothing.
  - Source: 10-08; signed 10-09 (the reset replaces "matches its look-alikes"). Built: No.
- **P6. Families of look-alikes** (Decided). A family's items earn no rewards until the owner confirms the family, shown as a large alert on the owner's page. The owner sets how many the family should sell per 90 days. Rewards then rise slowly toward that pace and never come down. Every confirmed family sits on a watch list: sales in the past period next to the floor count, back stock count and prices. The owner can change the pace, pause the family, or break it apart. When a family is broken apart, each item keeps the price it has that day and then moves on its own. Processing and AI watch the reward dynamics and the families; the owner decides.
  - Source: 10-08; signed 10-09 (replaces the automatic smooth rule). Built: Partly. Still open: how items move after a break-apart, and whether the rise is "memoryless". The owner calls these semi-firm and will settle them.
  - **Owner 10-13 (thrift_plus_families):** a possible family is the same product with 2+ floor units, or AI-matched similar products (within a subcategory). As soon as it's flagged it stops growing (it keeps the ThriftBucks it has; launch-day values stay) until the owner approves it with a sell target per 90 days or marks it not a family. Approved: one ThriftBucks % for all its units, rising 0.25% of the tag a night while it sells slower than the target (estimated conservatively: (sold + 1) ÷ days), holding at or above it, never down. Not a family: each unit grows on its own again. Every family is decided by launch ("Mark the rest not a family"). Built: Phase 1 (identical) v2.165.0; similar items next.

## Thrift+ credit

A member who tells the cashier redeems that trip's ThriftBucks instantly instead: at **50% of their value**, off today's price. One choice covers the whole trip (no splitting); it is logged. 50% is rounded to the cent in the member's favor. Not offered when Thrift+ credit covers the total (B3). The scanner shows only ThriftBucks; instant redemption is offered at the register (V8). The 50% is a setting.
  - Customer words: "Your ThriftBucks are saved automatically. Want them today? Tell the cashier: redeem them instantly at 50% of their value."

  - Source: Your picks 10-08; owner 10-09 (tell the cashier); owner 10-12 (50% replaces 80%; one noun). Built: No (built at 80%, as "InstantBucks").

- **B2. Two balances: ThriftBucks and Thrift+ credit** (Decided). Kept ThriftBucks are shown as "ThriftBucks", each amount with its use-by date.   - Customer words: "Your ThriftBucks: 35.00, use by Nov 19 · Thrift+ credit: $12.20"
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q16). Built: No.
- **B3. Spending: Thrift+ credit always first** (Decided). Thrift+ credit (refund money) is always used first, automatically, up to the whole total, as a payment after tax. Because ThriftBucks come off before tax, the register works them out so that the Thrift+ credit is spent in full first. If the Thrift+ credit covers the whole total, no ThriftBucks are used that trip: today's are kept in full and kept ones stay kept. Items bought with credit or ThriftBucks still collect ThriftBucks.
  - Customer words: "Thrift+ credit is always used first."
  - Source: Your rule 10-08. Built: No.
  - **Owner 10-14: kept ThriftBucks are used automatically** at the register, after the Thrift+ credit, soonest use-by first; the cashier has a one-tap "don't use them today". Built: v2.166.0 (one discount line before tax).

## Thrift+ 3-day returns (was the Limited Warranty; changed 10-12)

*What members can bring back, and what they get. Owner 10-12: "any reason, but only qualifying items". It replaces the Thrift+ Limited Warranty of 10-01 to 10-10: no reason is asked, nothing is tested, no arguing, little training.*

- **W1. What it is** (Decided 10-12). "Thrift+ 3-day returns", for members: a qualifying item can come back for **any reason** within 3 days. Staff don't ask why, don't test anything and don't judge the reason. It is a **return privilege, not a warranty**: the words "warranty" and "primary function" are not used, and every item is still sold AS IS as to its condition (W9, A3). The 20% the store keeps (W3) stops wear-once returns; the confidence to buy pricier, aging items is the point.
  - Customer words: "Thrift+ 3-day returns: any reason, on qualifying items. 80% back as Thrift+ credit."
  - Source: 10-01; signed 10-09 (the warranty); owner 10-12 (any reason, qualifying items only, 3 days; less labor and training; "the higher price people spend probably overshadows the fraud/waste"). Built: Partly (the return line is built; the qualifying-reason checks come out).
- **W2. How long** (Decided 10-12). 3 calendar days after the day of sale: bought on a Monday, back by Thursday. Nothing extends it. The one exception: when the store was closed, a manager may ask the owner, and only the owner may authorize a later return (O1).
  - Source: 10-07; signed 10-09 (7 days); owner 10-12 (3 days). The counting (sale day + 3) is Claude's; owner to confirm. Built: Yes (built for 3).
  - **Owner 10-14: confirmed, sale day + 3** (bought Monday, back by the end of Thursday).
- **W3. What they get back** (Decided; changed 10-12). 80% of everything they spent on the item, tax included, in the form they spent it: 80% of the money (and Thrift+ credit) as **Thrift+ credit, never back the way you paid** (owner 10-12: fewer refunds to payment cards and cancellations), which never expires, with a cash fallback only if the credit can't be used (the store closes or the membership ends); 80% of any ThriftBucks used on it, as ThriftBucks with a fresh 30 days.
  - Customer words: "80% of what you paid, tax included, back as Thrift+ credit."
  - Source: Your picks 10-08 (80%); owner 10-12 (never back the way you paid). Built: v2.166.0.
- **W4. ThriftBucks the returned item collected** (Decided). ## Sales tax and the books

*What is taxed, and when.*

- **T1. Instant redemption** (Decided; changed 10-12). The 50% comes off before tax: the item is taxed on the lower price.
  - Source: Follows F1. Built: No.
- **T2. Keeping ThriftBucks** (Decided). The item is taxed at the tag; creating reward credit is not a sale.
  - Source: Follows F1. Built: No.
- **T3. Spending ThriftBucks** (Decided). A discount on that later sale: tax is on the price after ThriftBucks. Recorded as a discount line, never as a tender, so the books show it as a reward.
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q16). Built: No.
- **T4. Refund credit** (Decided). The refund includes 80% of the tax, which the store takes back on its return. Spending refund credit later is a payment: that sale is taxed in full.
  - Source: Follows F2. Built: No.
- **T5. The register change** (Decided). Go: the register taxes by these rules, as part of the form 5 build.
  - Source: Claude, at your word 10-08. Built: No.
- **T6. The books** (Decided). Unused ThriftBucks and Thrift+ credit are liabilities until spent or expired (ASC 606 breakage); expired ThriftBucks are Thrift+ income (F4). Records per sale: tag, each discount by type (ThriftBucks spent, instant redemption), net taxable price, tax, each tender; per member, a ledger of rewards earned, used and expired; a monthly match of the register totals to each Form 10.
  - Source: Legal memo accepted by the CPA and attorney 10-01; your OK 10-08 (Q14, Q19). Built: No.
