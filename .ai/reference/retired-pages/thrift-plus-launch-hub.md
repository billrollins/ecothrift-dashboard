## The program in one page

**Joining and cards**
- Free; no cover and nothing owed (C1, C3)
- 18+, and up to 2 adults share one account and balance (J1)
- A card works only after a photo, name and phone, an ID check and a signature; start on the phone, finish at the register (J2, J8)
- Staff look at the ID and keep nothing from it; the photo is only for confirming the member, deleted 24 months after their last purchase (J3, J4)
- 18+ items need an ID check every time (J7)
- Email is optional; no texts (J9, X1)
- A lost card is stopped and replaced on the same account; nothing can be transferred (B7)

**ThriftBucks**
- On most items, but not an item's first 7 days on the floor, or back stock never seen on the floor (P1); consignment is in no customer words until consignment starts (P10)
- After a 7-day wait they grow 0.75% of the tag a day, up to 95% of the tag; the rate is never printed, the cap may be (P3, P8)
- At launch, floor stock counts at 30% of its real age, at most 45 days (P4)
- In back stock an item's age is frozen and reset to 0 (P5)
- Kept by default: usable from the next day through day 30, at full value (B1, B5)
- Or redeemed instantly at 50%, off today's price; the member tells the cashier, and one choice covers the whole trip (B1, T1)
- Spending order: Thrift+ credit first (after tax), then kept ThriftBucks, soonest use-by date first (before tax) (B3)
- No cash value, no fees, no cap (B4, B5)
- Thrift+ credit is only refund money, and it never expires (B2, F2)

**3-day returns and AS IS**
- For members, on qualifying items, for any reason, within 3 days after the day of sale; no questions and no testing (W1, W2)
- Back: 80% of what was paid, tax included, as Thrift+ credit, never to a card; cash only if the credit can't be used (W3)
- The member keeps 20% of the ThriftBucks the item collected (W4)
- Not returnable: AS IS on the tag or receipt, items marked as not qualifying, and anything damaged or heavily used (W6, W10, W7, A1)
- A Return button at the register; no swaps; settled on the return day (W11, W12, W13)
- Too many returns: staff may refuse, and the owner may limit returns or end the membership; never on posters (W14)
- A late return only when the store was closed, approved by the owner only (W2, O1)

**Guests**
- Pay the tag; every sale is AS IS and final (P1, W9)

**Families and the books (internal, never published)**
- Identical units, and later AI-matched similar ones, hold until the owner approves them with a sell target per 90 days (P6)
- An approved family: one % for all its units, rising slowly while sales are behind the target, never down (P6)
- A broken-apart family: each item keeps today's ThriftBucks (P6)
- An item is booked at its price minus its ThriftBucks; what instant redemptions leave and expired ThriftBucks are Thrift+ income (F4, T6)

  - "ThriftBucks collected 35.00, use by Nov 19" and "Your ThriftBucks: 35.00, use by Nov 19 · Thrift+ credit: $12.20" (V4, B2)
  - Each line marked "3-day return" or "AS IS"; guests get "All sales final. Sold AS IS." (A3, W9)
  - "ThriftBucks have no cash value." (B5)
- **Register:**
  - Opener: "Do you have a card yet?"
  - The choice: "Keep 35.00 ThriftBucks (Default) · or redeem instantly: $17.50 off today" (V4)
  - Rules: "Thrift+ credit is always used first." (B3) and "18+ items: ID checked every time." (J7)
  - Signature: "I agree to the Thrift+ terms and photo release. I can read them at ecothrift.us/terms." (J5)
- **Joining:** "Start your Thrift+ card on your phone, then finish at any register in a minute." · "18+. ID is used only for verification." · "Your photo protects your card balance." (J8, J3, J4)
- **Never say** (V2): discount (as the pitch), fee, dues, unlock, cash back, points, clawback, instant rebate, gift card, store credit, Thrift+ Cash, Member price, earn (say "collect"), bank or banked, use now, "the longer it waits", InstantBucks, Full reward, Instant reward, Rewards (as the name), cover, warranty, primary function. Never "Thrift Bucks", "TBucks" or "Bucks" alone. The only exceptions are "not a warranty" and "with no warranties" in the legal lines.

## Branding

- **Names:** Thrift+ is the membership. ThriftBucks is the only noun (one ThriftBuck): one word, capital T and B. "Redeem instantly" is the verb (V8).
- **Wordmark:** the brush Thrift+ logo everywhere (owner 10-15), "Thrift" in white and "+" in #5fd34a on the app header. Black Anemone is dropped.
- **Palette** (`scannerTheme.tsx`):
  - Greens: #1d5424 titles · #1f6b27 prices and numbers · #3f9f35 member green, buttons, the "+" · #52b843 bright · #237a26 deep
  - #e7f3e0 tint · #f2f2ee page · #2a2d28 ink
- **Fonts:** the scanner uses Roboto, Roboto Condensed 700 and Nunito 900 (numbers); the posters use Manrope, Nunito and Spectral.
- **ThriftBucks amounts:** always a number next to the word ("35.00 ThriftBucks"), never "$" and never the TB mark. Prices, tax, totals and Thrift+ credit stay in dollars. The certificate art keeps its mark (it's a picture) and always carries its terms QR.
- **Posters:**
  - Three 13 x 19 posters in a row (Meet | ThriftBucks | Returns) under one sky
  - Guest in white, member in #3f9f35
  - Text, QR codes and diagrams in vector; AI art only where it reads as a real photo
  - Body type at least 14.5 pt and a 0.6 in safe area, with each string kept inside its slot budget (08 §5)
  - One big number, not two
- **QR codes:** each one is checked by decoding it from the PDF. The terms QR is 1.06 in ("TERMS"); the join QR is 1.75 in. The pages must be live before printing: /terms (every footer, the certificate), /join (poster 1), /scan (poster 2).
- **Spanish:** one small line, last in the footer, the same on all three posters: "En español: Thrift+ es gratis. Escanee aquí para español o visite ecothrift.us/terms →" (V5)
