## 5. FAQ

**Is Thrift+ really free?**
Yes. There's nothing to pay to get your card. Each month, the first 10 ThriftBucks you collect cover your card, and everything after that is yours. If you collect fewer than 10 in a month, they simply cover less. You never owe anything, and you keep every Thrift+ benefit either way.

**What exactly is "the cover"?**
It's how the card is covered: the first 10 ThriftBucks you collect each calendar month go to it. It starts fresh on the 1st and never carries over.

**How do ThriftBucks grow?**
Most items collect ThriftBucks that grow the longer they sit in the store, up to 95% of the tag. Eco-Thrift sets them, and they can change from day to day. Scan any tag with the Thrift+ scanner to see today's ThriftBucks on it.

**What's the difference between ThriftBucks and InstantBucks?**
Keeping your **ThriftBucks** is the Default: they're saved to your account automatically, in full, and you can use them from the next day for 30 days. If you'd rather use them right away, tell the cashier and take **InstantBucks**: 80% of your ThriftBucks, off today's price. One choice covers the whole trip.

**When do my ThriftBucks expire?**
The ThriftBucks from each trip are good from the day after the trip through 30 days. Your receipts and My account show every use-by date, soonest first.

**Can I use ThriftBucks on anything?**
On almost everything. ThriftBucks can't be used on consignment items. Thrift+ credit can.

**Something I bought doesn't work. What do I do?**
If the item is marked "Limited Warranty" on your receipt and in the scanner, and its primary function doesn't work, bring it to any register within 7 days after the day you bought it, with your Thrift+ card or phone number. You get 80% of what you paid, tax included, back as Thrift+ credit.

**What doesn't the warranty cover?**
Clothing and soft goods, consumables, parts, items without a primary function, items marked AS IS, and items $10 or under after ThriftBucks. It also doesn't cover a change of mind, scratches and wear, condition, missing accessories, or extra features.

**Can I swap a return for something else?**
No swaps. A warranty return goes back to you as Thrift+ credit, which never expires, and you can spend it on anything in the store.

**I lost my card.**
Come to any register. We'll stop the old card and link a new one to the same account, so nothing is lost.

**Can my partner share my membership?**
Yes. Up to 2 adults can share one membership and one balance. Both need to be at the register to add the second adult.

**Do you keep my ID or photo?**
We look at your photo ID once to check your name and age, and we keep nothing from it. Your photo is used only so staff can confirm it's you. It's never used for marketing or shared, and it's deleted 24 months after your last purchase, or sooner if you ask.

**Will you text me?**
No. If you give us an email, we send receipts and Thrift+ updates. Store news emails always have an unsubscribe link.

**Can I turn ThriftBucks into cash?**
No. ThriftBucks have no cash value. Thrift+ credit isn't cash either, except where the warranty or the law says so.

**Thrift+ Membership Terms.** Eco-Thrift LLC, 8425 West Center Road, Omaha, NE 68124 · (402) 881-9861 · Version: [date].

### §1 Joining

1.1 Thrift+ is a free membership program of Eco-Thrift LLC ("Eco-Thrift", "we"). You must be 18 or older to join. [J1]

1.2 Up to 2 adults may share one membership and one account. Both must be present at a register to add the second adult, and the first adult approves. [J1]

1.3 A card works only after we have your photo, your name and phone number, a check of your photo ID, and your signature on the register screen. Until then, the card collects and uses nothing. You may start on your phone at ecothrift.us/join and finish at any register. [J2, J8]

1.4 By signing, you agree to these terms and to the photo release in §10. [J5]

1.5 Items for adults 18 and over require an ID check every time, member or not. [J7]

### §2 The monthly cover

2.1 The first 10 ThriftBucks you collect each calendar month cover your card. The cover comes out of your ThriftBucks first, before the choice between keeping them and InstantBucks. It resets on the 1st of each month and never rolls over. In a month with fewer than 10 ThriftBucks, it covers less. [C1]

2.2 Nothing is ever owed for the cover, and an uncovered month takes away no benefit. [C5]

2.3 ThriftBucks that go to the cover don't lower an item's price; the item is taxed at the tag. [C4]

### §3 ThriftBucks

3.1 Members collect ThriftBucks on most items. Consignment items don't carry ThriftBucks. Neither does an item in its first 7 days on the sales floor, or back stock that has not yet been on the floor. [P1]

3.2 ThriftBucks grow as an item sits, up to 95% of its tag price. ThriftBucks are set by Eco-Thrift, can change from day to day, and are shown in the Thrift+ scanner and at the register. [P3, P8]

### §4 ThriftBucks and InstantBucks

4.1 **ThriftBucks kept (Default).** Your ThriftBucks on a trip are saved to your account in full, automatically. They can be used from the day after the trip through the 30th day after it, and then they expire. [B1, B5, V8]

4.2 **InstantBucks.** If you tell the cashier, you may instead take InstantBucks: 80% of that trip's ThriftBucks, taken off that day's price and rounded to the cent in your favor. One choice covers the whole trip. [B1, V8]

4.3 InstantBucks are not offered when Thrift+ credit covers the whole total (§5). [B3]

### §5 Using ThriftBucks and Thrift+ credit

5.1 Thrift+ credit is always used first, automatically, as a payment after tax. Saved ThriftBucks apply to what is left, soonest use-by date first, as a reduction in price before tax. [B3, T3]

5.2 ThriftBucks can't be used on consignment items; Thrift+ credit can. Items bought with ThriftBucks or Thrift+ credit still collect ThriftBucks. [B3]

5.3 Your ThriftBucks, their use-by dates and your Thrift+ credit are shown on your receipts and in My account. [B13]

### §6 No cash value; no transfers; lost cards

6.1 ThriftBucks are a loyalty reward from Eco-Thrift, not something you buy. ThriftBucks have no cash value. [B5, V8]

6.2 ThriftBucks and Thrift+ credit can't be sold, traded or transferred to anyone outside your membership. [B7]

6.3 There are no fees. [B5]

6.4 If your card is lost, we stop it and link a new card to the same account. [B7]

### §7 Thrift+ credit

7.1 Thrift+ credit is the money part of a Limited Warranty refund (§8). It never expires. [B2, B5]

7.2 Thrift+ credit can't be exchanged for cash, except as §8.6 provides or where the law requires. Thrift+ credit unused for 3 years is reported to the Nebraska State Treasurer as the law requires. [B5, B11]

### §8 Thrift+ Limited Warranty

8.1 **Who is covered.** The Thrift+ member who bought the item with their card. [W1]

8.2 **What is covered.** Items marked "Limited Warranty" on your receipt and in the scanner whose **primary function** doesn't work: the main thing the item is made for (a blender blends; a mower mows). We can't test most items; this warranty covers them anyway. [W1, W10, A3]

8.3 **What is not covered.**
- Clothing and soft goods; consumables; parts; items without a primary function.
- Items marked AS IS or NO THRIFT+ WARRANTY.
- Items whose price after ThriftBucks was $10 or less.
- A non-primary function; missing accessories; condition, scratches or wear; a change of mind.

[W1, W6]

8.4 **How long.** 7 calendar days after the day of sale. Bought on a Monday? Bring it back by the next Monday. The period isn't extended for a customer's reasons. [W2]

8.5 **How to make a claim.**
1. Bring the item to any register within 7 days with your Thrift+ card or the phone number on your account.
2. Staff check that its primary function doesn't work.
3. If we say no, you may ask a manager to review.

[W7, W13]

8.6 **What we will do.** At no charge to you:
- **What you paid:** we give 80% of what you paid for the item, tax included, as Thrift+ credit, which never expires.
- **ThriftBucks you used on it:** 80% of any ThriftBucks you used come back as ThriftBucks with a fresh 30 days.
- **ThriftBucks the item collected:** you keep 20% of them. We take back the other 80%: first from this month's cover, then from your unspent ThriftBucks. If those were already spent, your credit is reduced by that amount; if they already expired, nothing is taken.
- **When it's settled:** the return is settled on the day you bring it back, and no earlier receipt changes.
- **No swaps:** a return is never an exchange for another item.
- **Cash:** if we can't provide usable Thrift+ credit (for example, if the store closes or your membership ends), we refund the same amount in cash.

This is your sole and exclusive remedy. [W3, W4, W11, W12, F2]
