## Build notes for the page

- **Address:** `ecothrift.us/terms` (every poster's footer QR).
  - The older addresses `ecothrift.us/thriftplus` and `/thriftplus/warranty` (J5, W8) should redirect there.
  - The Limited Warranty section's anchor is `#s8`.
- **Chart:** each row's number (A.1 …) links to its section (`#s1` … `#s12`). The full terms sit below in smaller, quieter type.
- **Buttons:** big "Open the Scanner" (`/scan`) and "Join Thrift+" (`/join`), each with its QR code, so any poster QR leads anywhere.
- **Toggle:** English | Español at the top right, remembered on the phone.
- **Perks:** show only live perks (M1 now). Add M2 to M4 when they are built; never before.
- **IDs:** the `[J1]`-style IDs in the files are for the coder and reviewers. Don't print them.
- **Words:** follow the rulebook's word rules (ThriftBucks and InstantBucks per V8, "Default", "collect", "covers your card"; amounts as a number next to the word, never "$" or a symbol; never "bank", "earn", "Member price", "store credit", "gift card", "points").
- **Scanner explainer:** the Thrift+ explainer popup (owner 10-10) uses "all you need to know" (block 2) and links to `/terms`.
