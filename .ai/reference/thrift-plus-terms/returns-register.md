# THRIFT+ 3-DAY RETURNS

EcoThrift LLC · 8425 West Center Road, Omaha, NE 68124 · (402) 881-9861 · Version: [date]

**Who.** The Thrift+ member who bought the item on their account.

**What can be returned.** Items your receipt marks "3-day return", for any reason. We don't ask why, and we don't test items. Items marked AS IS on the tag or receipt can't be returned.

**How long.** 3 calendar days after the day of sale. Bought on a Monday? Bring it back by Thursday. The period isn't extended for a customer's reasons.

**How.**
1. Bring the item to any register within 3 days with the member QR in your Thrift+ app or the phone number on your account.
2. It must be the item from that trip, not broken, damaged or heavily used after the sale.
3. If we say no, you may ask a manager to review.

**What you get back.**
- **What you paid:** 80% of what you paid for the item, tax included, as Thrift+ credit, which never expires. It is never refunded to the way you paid.
- **ThriftBucks you used on it:** 80% of any ThriftBucks you used come back as ThriftBucks with a fresh 30 days.
- **ThriftBucks the item collected:** you keep 20% of them. We take back the other 80% from your unspent ThriftBucks. If those were already spent, your credit is reduced by that amount; if they already expired, nothing is taken.
- **When it's settled:** the return is settled on the day you bring it back, and no earlier receipt changes.
- **No swaps:** a return is never an exchange for another item.
- **Cash:** if we can't provide usable Thrift+ credit (for example, if the store closes or your membership ends), we refund the same amount in cash.

**Not a warranty.** 3-day returns are a return privilege for members. They are not a warranty, and every item is sold AS IS.

**Too many returns.** We keep a record of returns. If a member returns items too often, or misuses returns, we may limit or refuse their returns, or end their membership.

**AS IS sales.** Every item is sold AS IS, with all faults, with no warranties, express or implied, including merchantability. Non-members: all sales final. Members may return qualifying items as above.
