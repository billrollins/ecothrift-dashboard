Blog Studio

Pick a typography & color group

Five cohesive systems for the blog's display type (the post title and drop cap), headings, body text, accent / link color, and the highlight wash. Each one swaps 2–3 fonts plus a color direction so the “big letter at the front” and the headers all feel like one family.

Tell me the number you want (e.g. “go with 02”) and I'll wire it into the editor and the public post page. Colors are easy to nudge afterward — these are starting points, not locked in.
01Bookish Calm
Spectral display · Manrope body
Field Notes

The quiet economics of buying things twice

By Bill Rollins · 6 min read · Sustainability

Every pallet that crosses our dock started life somewhere else — a return, an overstock, a closeout no one had time to sort. The work is deciding what still has a second life in it.

What the manifest doesn't say

Some weeks the numbers look thin until you actually open the boxes. The real resale value hides in the details a spreadsheet flattens: condition, brand, and the small things that make something worth keeping. You can read more in last month's recap.

Reuse isn't nostalgia — it's the most efficient supply chain we have.

So we sort by hand, photograph the good stuff, and let the rest tell us where the margins really are.
