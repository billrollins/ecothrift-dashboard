# Retail: end goal (draft v1, 2026-11-06)

**Status:** draft for Bill. Locked once he says yes. After that it changes only when Bill says "change the goal", logged in the Q&A log. Sessions never change it.

**The workspace's job:** check customers out fast and right, handle returns and Thrift+ members on the spot, and account for every dollar of cash from open to bank.

## The finished menu

| # | Page | Its one job | It will NOT |
|---|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Register** | Ring a sale, take a return (Return button), attach a member, take payment, print the receipt. | Hold reports, setup, or drawer counting beyond open, cover and close. |
| 2 | **Transactions** | Find any checkout or return (receipt, member, cashier, date); open it; reprint. | Edit sales; be a report. |
| 3 | **Customers** | Find a Thrift+ member, see their ThriftBucks, returns and visits, fix their name or phone, finish a stuck join. | Run marketing or Thrift+ settings. |
| — | *Managers* | | |
| 4 | **Command Center** | Today's floor at a glance: sales vs goal, who is on, open drawers, what needs a manager now. | Become the owner's page. |
| 5 | **Cash Drops** | Every dollar of cash: drawers, covers and handovers, drops into the box, the office drawer and safe swaps, the bank deposit. | Do payroll or accounting books. |
| 6 | **Retail setup** | Registers, printers, receipt and Thrift+ settings, practice register. | Show up in a cashier's daily menu. |

Floor shortcuts (pages owned by inventory, shown here because the floor uses them): Search, Run count, Review, Tag errors, Quick reprice.

## How a page gets built (all pages)

1. A one-page spec from this goal: its job, what is on it, what it will not do. Bill says yes.
2. Build to the spec. The critic grades against the spec and this goal, at most 3 rounds.
3. New ideas during the build go on the page's **Next version** list below, not into the build.
4. Bill uses it and signs off: it moves to Finished.
