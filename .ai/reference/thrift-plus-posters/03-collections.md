# Collections

Four collections, each with its own philosophy, so the owner sees real range. All four stay on brand: the public-site colours and the Spectral and Manrope typefaces. Collection D also uses the scanner's greens, because the posters send people to the scanner.

Shared craft rules for every collection:
- **Hierarchy.** At most four levels: headline, lead, body, fine print.
- **Reading distance.**
  - Headlines are at least 1 in cap height, readable from 10 ft.
  - Body text is at least 24 pt.
  - Fine print is at least 15 pt.
  - The warranty terms are at least 20 pt, with no tiny legal type.
- **Type.**
  - Two typefaces at most, with no faux bold or italic.
  - Body lines run 45 to 75 characters.
  - Headlines are balanced, with no widows.
  - Curly quotes, and no em or en dashes.
- **Safe area.** All live content sits at least 0.6 in from the trim, so a home printer's edge loss never cuts anything. Backgrounds bleed to the trim.
- **One light direction per poster** (upper left) across every photograph on it.
- **Images.** Every AI image either melts into the page or is cut out on purpose. No AI image sits in a plain rectangle like a pasted snapshot.

## A. Specimen

**"Every find deserves a museum label."** Thrift finds are photographed and labelled like specimens on a calm, premium editorial page.

- **Goal.**
  - Make secondhand feel chosen and valuable.
  - Quiet authority that suits rules and a warranty.
  - It flatters the merchandise, which is the store's real hero.
- **Mood.** Museum catalog, Kinfolk, gallery wall label. Calm, light, generous space.
- **Type.**
  - Spectral Light and Regular for display: 110 to 220 pt, tracking -0.02 em, leading 0.95.
  - Spectral italic for one aside per poster.
  - Manrope 600 small capitals for labels: 0.14 em tracking, 14 to 18 pt.
  - Manrope 400 and 500 for body.
  - Catalog numbers in Spectral old-style figures ("No. 07").
- **Colour.**
  - Page `#f4f6f5` dominates, about 70%. Ink type.
  - Forest `#18452d` for accents and at most one solid band.
  - Photographs keep their natural tones, and the photo backdrop is matched to the page colour exactly.
- **Grid.** 12 columns, 0.75 in margins, 12 pt baseline, asymmetric.
- **Imagery.**
  - Still-life photographs of real-looking secondhand objects (brass lamp, ceramic pitcher, chair, blender, toaster, wool sweater, film camera, radio), from the pro model.
  - Each object is shot on a seamless backdrop. We colour-match that backdrop to the page and feather it out, so the object stands on the poster with its own shadow.
  - Cutouts can stand in front of the headline for depth.
- **Signature devices.**
  - Hairline leader lines from an object to its label.
  - Specimen captions and catalog numbers.
  - Thin rules, and a museum-label card.
- **Do.** Whitespace, patina and wear, one hero object or one deliberate plate of several, sentence-case captions.
- **Don't.** Boxes around photos, drop shadows on type, a second accent colour, busy backdrops, glossy "product render" looks.

## B. System

**"The rules, drawn so anyone gets them in five seconds."** Swiss information design: the program as a small, clear system.

- **Goal.**
  - The clearest possible explanation: join, earn, choose, protected.
  - It suits the all-in-one poster and the Member vs Guest comparisons best.
- **Mood.** Transit signage, an annual-report infographic, a good instruction manual.
- **Type.**
  - Manrope 800 for headlines: tracking -0.025 em, leading 0.95.
  - Manrope 500 for body.
  - Big numerals (80%, 100%, 30, 7, $10) in Manrope 800 at 180 to 400 pt.
  - One Spectral italic line per poster for the human voice ("Love it? It might be gone tomorrow.").
- **Colour.**
  - Solid fields: forest blocks with white type, tint `#ebf1ed` panels.
  - Ink `#1a1f1c` for Guest and SOLD AS IS.
  - No gradients.
- **Grid.** Strict 6 columns, 0.7 in margins, flush left, modules on a 0.25 in rhythm.
- **Imagery.**
  - Vector icons drawn for this set: one stroke weight, round caps.
  - At most one photo plate per poster, turned into a two-colour halftone (forest dots on tint) and rendered at 600 dpi. The dots stay crisp at any size, and the AI source never shows its softness.
- **Signature devices.** Flow arrows, the two-path fork, calendar strips (7 cells, 30 cells), percentage bars, numbered modules, the "+" as a super-graphic.
- **Do.** Labels on every diagram part, consistent strokes, alignment you can rule a line through.
- **Don't.** Decoration without meaning, more than two colours in a module, an untreated photo.

## C. Tag

**"The price tag is the hero."** A bold retail campaign built from the store's most familiar object, the paper price tag.

- **Goal.**
  - Stop people from across the aisle.
  - Instantly about prices and Rewards.
  - Playful but clean.
- **Mood.** A confident retail campaign, tactile and graphic. Kraft paper, string and rubber stamps.
- **Type.**
  - Manrope 800 capitals for headlines (tracking 0.01 em).
  - Spectral italic for warm asides.
  - Manrope 600 for body.
- **Colour.**
  - Forest, white tags and ink.
  - Kraft brown comes from the paper texture itself, as a material, not a new brand colour.
- **Imagery.**
  - Macro textures of kraft card stock and thermal receipt paper. These are generated, made seamless and tiled at 400 dpi or more.
  - A few photographic tags tied to objects, clipped inside tag shapes.
  - Everything else is vector: tag shapes with punched holes and reinforcement rings, string drawn as a vector path, and a rubber-stamp "SOLD AS IS" with a real ink texture rendered at 600 dpi.
- **Signature devices.** Oversized tags cropped by the page edge, perforated tear-off stubs, a string that links the steps, a stamped SOLD AS IS, sticker dots.
- **Do.** Big shapes, a tilt of 8 degrees at most, real paper tactility, plenty of contrast.
- **Don't.** A clutter of little tags, handwriting fonts, crumpled-paper clichés, fake distress on the type.

## D. Field

**"The Thrift+ app, printed."** Bright, friendly and fresh, matching the price scanner shoppers open on their phones.

- **Goal.** Make the poster and the scanner feel like one product. Optimistic and energetic.
- **Mood.** The scanner's morning field, white rounded cards, soft daylight.
- **Type.**
  - Manrope 800 headlines and Manrope 500 body. The scanner's Roboto is a screen face, not a poster face.
  - The Thrift+ brush wordmark is the hero mark. It must first be rebuilt at print size, and the owner must confirm it is the official mark.
- **Colour.**
  - Scanner greens: `#3f9f35` and `#52b843` accents, `#1d5424` titles.
  - White cards on page `#f2f2ee`, and forest for long text.
  - No gold coins.
- **Imagery.**
  - Sunlit field and soft green-bokeh plates generated at 2k. Soft subjects hold up at about 130 dpi, and they are finished with print grain at 300 dpi.
  - A phone drawn in vector, with the scanner screen rebuilt in HTML. It stays sharp at any size and is never a screenshot.
  - Object photographs as rounded cards, cut to a soft-cornered shape with the backdrop matched.
- **Signature devices.** Rounded cards with soft shadows (like the scanner), pill buttons, the "Use today | Save" toggle drawn like the register choice, the brush wordmark.
- **Do.** Generous rounding, airy spacing, UI drawn as precisely as the real app.
- **Don't.** Coins, confetti, sparkles, neon, game-like badges.
