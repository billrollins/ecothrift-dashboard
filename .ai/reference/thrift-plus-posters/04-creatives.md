# Creatives (as built)

The owner's two paths, plus the decided sign set, give five creatives:
- **Path 1** is K1 alone.
- **Path 2** is K2, K3 and K4, which is exactly the rulebook's A4 set: Meet Thrift+, Prices, Returns.
- **K5** goes up with either path, because excluded categories need an AS IS notice before the sale (W6, A4).

| ID | Creative | Where it hangs | Size |
|----|----------|----------------|------|
| K1 | **All-in-one**: "Every find earns Rewards." Join, earn, use or save, warranty, all disclaimers | Entrance (the only poster on path 1) | Variation 1 is 13 x 19 portrait, variation 2 is 19 x 13 landscape, variation 3 is 18 x 24 (print shop) |
| K2 | **Meet Thrift+**: what it is, how to join, all disclaimers | Entrance | 13 x 19 |
| K3 | **Prices: Member vs Guest**: use 80% today or save 100% for 30 days; scan any tag | Racks and aisles | 13 x 19 |
| K4 | **Returns: Member vs Guest**: the Thrift+ Limited Warranty and SOLD AS IS | Every register | 13 x 19 |
| K5 | **Area signs**: NO THRIFT+ WARRANTY. Sold as is to everyone. Final sale. | Clothing, 18+, crossbows | 11 x 8.5 landscape, one page per area |

Every variation passes the automatic QA (05-production) and a visual review.

PDFs are in `workspace/posters2/out/<collection>/<id>.pdf`, and each collection is combined in `out/Thrift+ posters - Collection <X> <Name> - review.pdf`.
