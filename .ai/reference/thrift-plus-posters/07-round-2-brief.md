# Round 2 brief (owners' review, 2026-10-09)

Bill and Carrie reviewed the 60 round-1 posters. This is their direction for round 2. Words come from the rulebook ([`../../extended/thrift-plus-decisions.md`](../../extended/thrift-plus-decisions.md)), signed 10-09.

## The set

- **Three portrait posters, hung in a row, left to right:** Meet Thrift+ | Rewards | Limited Warranty.
- **One family: D (Field), because it matches the scanner app.** Readability of the D fonts is liked, and C's fonts are liked too.
- **Continuous but distinct:** the three read as one wall, but are not carbon copies. Embrace portrait, and design them collectively as a row.
- **The scanner app** will change a little, so the posters must follow it.

## Every poster

- **The same title look:** a standard title block on all three.
- **The same fine print:** short. One clean separator line at the bottom, a few to-the-point lines, and a QR code to the full terms. The same footer on all three. No massive fine print (the worst problem in round 1).
- **Logo:** white, with a shadow (or similar) so it pops off the page and never washes out. The logo's fonts are Verdana Bold and Verdana Regular.
- **Tags:** blank cardboard, always hanging landscape. Real printed labels get stuck on top, and customers read them without turning their head.
- **Guest vs Rewards:** this comparison is the purpose of the posters. Each poster shows it in its own way.
- **White space** wherever possible; the purpose must be clear at a glance.
- **No script or brush type.** The scripty "Every find earns Rewards" on D-K2-2 is out.
- **New words:**
  - Instant reward and Full reward ("Instant is 80% of your Full reward");
  - the cover ("Your first $10 in Rewards each month covers your card");
  - Limited Warranty: 7 days, the primary function, qualifying items, and the qualifying reason (better words welcome).

## Per poster (what they liked)

- **Meet Thrift+, based on D-K2-2 (the closest of all).**
  - Kept: the phone, which becomes a real scanner screenshot when it is ready. The phone could appear on all three.
  - Kept: the separated cards, and the "what now" part looking different from the three.
  - Add numbers to the three cards, in this order: Your Card, Rewards, Limited Warranty. Then the How (to join).
  - Adjust the numbers, the phone size and the detail size, and change the logo (white, see above).
- **Rewards, based on D-K3-3** with the clarity of B-K3-1's chart.
  - Drop the two bubbles at the bottom: the bottom becomes the clean footer.
  - This leaves the "Pays the tag / Earns Rewards" comparison as the core.
- **Limited Warranty, looking like D-K4-3.**
  - D-K4-2's guest-vs-member chart is a good scanner-style idea, but it has too much text, its type is too small and it feels cluttered.
  - C-K4-3's receipt says "returns" instantly. Bill likes it; Carrie doesn't, so it needs revising if used.
- **Also liked:**
  - C's cardboard colour and texture, and its tags as a way to separate steps without drowning attention (C-K1-1, C-K2-2);
  - A-K2-1's general look, though it may not fit the brand.

## The terms page (a separate build)

The QR on every poster opens one page: the Thrift+ terms. It has:
- an English/Spanish toggle;
- a fun, to-the-point "all you need to know";
- one simple chart of every detail, each row numbered (A.1 …). Tapping a number jumps to the legal section that covers it in full, including one-off cases;
- a plain-language FAQ;
- the full legal text, deliberately less eye-catching.

## Round 2 plan (owner said go, 10-09)

1. **Words first (Claude, no review round).** Rewrite the copy deck (`02-copy-deck.md`) to the signed rulebook, plus the standard footer.
2. **Wall proofs.** One landscape image: the three posters side by side, at true proportion, built from shared parts (title block, logo, card, numbered badge, tag, chart, footer). Two versions of the wall at most, differing only on the open choices. Screen resolution, reusing existing art, with no new AI images. A 100% crop of the footer goes with it.
3. **Owners pick and mark up.** A change to a shared part updates all three posters.
4. **Finals.** Full-size print PDFs with the full QA (05-production), plus one home test print before printing the set.
